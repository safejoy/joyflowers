<p>Hi there! I&rsquo;m Joy Flowers. 🌸</p>
<p class="">Welcome to my little corner of the internet! I&rsquo;m an artist, game developer, and storyteller who loves creating cozy, colorful worlds where everyone can feel seen and celebrated. 🦊✨</p>
<p class="">I&rsquo;m a proud <strong>trans woman</strong> and an all-around lover of life&rsquo;s small joys&mdash;like sipping coffee on rainy mornings, curling up with my cats, and getting lost in a sketchbook full of new ideas. Creativity has always been my way of connecting with myself and others, and I pour my heart into every piece of art I create.</p>
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What I Do</strong> 🎨</h3>
<p class="">I specialize in soft, whimsical artwork with a splash of magic&mdash;anthropomorphic characters, fantasy settings, and LGBTQIA+ themes are my jam! My art often feels like a love letter to self-discovery, cozy emotions, and finding beauty in the everyday.</p>
<p class="">Right now, I&rsquo;m also working on my very own <strong>visual novel game</strong>, <em>Becoming Joy</em>&mdash;a story about identity, growth, and learning to embrace who you are. It&rsquo;s a project close to my heart, and I hope it can inspire and comfort others on their own journeys.</p>
